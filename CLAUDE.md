# Jensen Joyride

Parody jetpack runner (canvas 2D, plain ES modules, no build). Read `README.md`.

- All art is hand-coded in `src/draw.js`; no copied assets, characters or names from other games.
- Bosses come from the public business story only. Never add private people (partners, spouse, family) as
  characters, bosses, likenesses or jokes, and nothing sexual. Keep the parody label and disclaimer.
- Look: Press Cycle editorial style (cream paper, ink, red `#e8352b`, yellow `#ffc93c`, Unbounded / Geist / Geist Mono).
- Own repo and own Vercel project `jensen-joyride`. Production deploys from `main` only; batch changes,
  no preview branches.
